How a nook gets made

Six steps, about eleven weeks for a two-bedroom flat.

  1. First we visit

    Two hours in your home, with a tape and a notebook. We ask how mornings go.

  2. Then we listen

    Coffee at the studio. You bring photos of rooms you love, we bring none.

  3. We draw it whole

    The house as one model, measured to the centimetre, every window placed.

  4. We take it apart

    Room by room, until each corner has a reason: read, cook, rest, store.

  5. We build it

    Our carpenters and painters, on site weekly. You get a photo every Friday.

  6. You move in

    We style the last shelf, water the plants and leave you the keys.
